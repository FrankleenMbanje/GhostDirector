# Celebrity media policy

### Hybrid review edits

When clean licensed footage is available, a hybrid may combine at least two genuine video originals with at least four distinct genuine photographs. At least six independent visual originals must appear, genuine moving footage must cover at least 30% of the measured edit, each source remains capped, and source intervals cannot overlap. This is a separate mix from the six-photo fallback and classic three-video-original edit. No generic stock, printed likeness of the subject, brand-marked frame, or source duplicate may be used to fill the count. Editorial shortlists still pass the same license and individual visual checks.
